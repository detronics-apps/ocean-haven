BlueHaven

Start with one little island. Help the ocean around you. Watch it come alive.

Play BlueHaven

What you do

  • Sail your little boat and collect the litter washing into the sea.
  • Calmly help sea turtles, dolphins and crabs, and fill your Ocean Journal.
  • Build protected beaches where turtles nest and hatchlings reach the sea.
  • Recycle litter, earn conservation funding and grow your island.
  • Set sail for new islands once your home is looked after.

Keep your progress on a phone

The game saves by itself. To make sure your phone keeps it, add BlueHaven to your home screen and always open it from there:

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Play BlueHaven in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open BlueHaven from its new icon.

Android

  1. Open Play BlueHaven in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open BlueHaven from its new icon.

Progress is kept on each device separately. Private browsing, or clearing your browser's data, removes it.

BlueHaven is a game in progress. Turn your phone sideways to play.
